# Inclusive Language Checklist (Req 18.4)

All displayed narrative and labels are reviewed against these principles so the
journey welcomes every visitor and avoids stereotyping cultures or cuisines.

## Principles applied
- **Describe, don't rank.** Cuisines are presented as different, not "better" or
  "exotic." We avoid words like exotic, primitive, weird, or authentic-as-gatekeeping.
- **People-first, culture-respectful.** We refer to countries and regions neutrally;
  we do not attribute traits to peoples. Food traditions are framed as shared human
  practices ("how the world lives"), not curiosities.
- **No stereotyping shortcuts.** Narrative avoids reducing a nation to a single dish
  or cliché; dishes are examples, not definitions.
- **Plain, accessible wording.** Sentences are short and concrete; jargon is avoided
  or explained. Numbers include units and context.
- **Data honesty.** Where data is missing we say "Not available" rather than implying
  absence of the thing itself.

## Where this is enforced
- `components/narrative.py` — section opening sentences and discovery insights.
- `components/navigation.py` — section labels.
- Section modules — captions, headings, and empty-state messages.
